Frequently asked questions

Do I need an iPhone or an Android?

There is an Android app. On iPhone, computer or tablet, everything works in the browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Do my employees pay?

No. Only the company owner has a subscription. Employees, managers and temps use the app for free.

How do people sign in?

With a Google account, no new password. One account for all your companies. Once your Telegram is linked, you can also ask the bot for a sign-in link (/connexion) and open Staff Flow in any browser, with nothing to install.

What if the phone has no signal?

The schedule you already received stays on screen. The owner can keep planning and an employee can ask for leave: it is kept in the phone's memory and sent as soon as the connection is back. Others see those changes only then.

Is my data protected?

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and so are backups. Your schedules and announcements are never sold or used for advertising.

Does Staff Flow connect to my payroll software or CRM?

Yes, with the API access option: your software automatically reads the published schedule, hours per employee and absences, as JSON or CSV. You create the keys yourself in the app and can revoke them at any time. The documentation is public: staff-flow.cloud/en/api.

Can I manage several companies?

Yes: one tab per company. Someone who works for two companies sees all their schedules in one place.

How do I import all my employees at once?

In Configuration, tap the import icon, download the Excel template and fill in one row per employee: full name (required), employee number, position and site (optional). Save it as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, then pick it in the app. A preview shows every row before you confirm: new, updated, already invited or with an error and its reason. Missing positions and sites are created automatically.

Then how do my employees join the company?

Each one gets a personal code and QR code: the “Pending invitations” page shows them and can print them all on one sheet. On the sign-in screen, the employee taps “Activate my account”, scans their QR code or types their code, then links an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator…): no Google account or e-mail needed. After that, they sign in with their SF ID or employee number and the app's 6-digit code, and can stay signed in on their phone. An employee who already uses Google can also choose “I have an invitation code”. An unused invitation is erased after 90 days.

Which phones are supported?

The Android app works from Android 7 (2016). On an older phone, an iPhone or a computer, use Staff Flow in an up-to-date browser at app.staff-flow.cloud.
